[Research Project Two — Topic]

When geopolitics flares, semiconductor stocks do not drift — they jump. I built a Python pipeline that simulates 20-day returns of the PHLX Semiconductor Index under jump-diffusion models whose intensity is conditioned on the Geopolitical Risk (GPR) index, benchmarked against GBM, Heston, and fractional Brownian motion. Trained on 1994–2019 and stress-tested on 2020–2026, the models are scored by how well their tail-risk (VaR) forecasts hold up in high- and low-risk regimes.
