Soccer — [Team Name]

I joined varsity a year early — a freshman when the coach, short on defenders, walked over and asked me to step up. Four seasons on, I have started every match at center back and played every minute. As the last defender on the line, I am the one the team counts on when a striker comes through. We compete in Division 1 of the Shanghai High School Soccer Association League.

Three seasons, three championships — and in half of our games the opposition never took a single shot. I kept the back line safe and still added three goals of my own. Twice I broke my leg in the offseason and couldn't train; both times I kept showing up anyway, supporting my teammates from the sideline. That is where I learned what captaincy really is: not controlling a match, but uniting a team, on and off the pitch.
